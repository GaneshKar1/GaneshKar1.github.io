field note 01 · shepherd ventures, 2026

the best result i ever had was wrong.

At Shepherd I research portfolios that are built to hold up when the market crashes. One configuration looked incredible: in its worst crash it still avoided almost half of the market's drop.

It bothered me. So I checked every input against the crashes themselves, and one of them was too good. A forward-looking data field correlated 0.77 with the crash returns. The strongest honest signal we had, momentum, sat at 0.41. The data was quietly leaking the future into the backtest.

Taking it out cut the worst-case number in half. Nobody enjoys deleting their best result. I'd still rather know.
